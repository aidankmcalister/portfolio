# Home (one-page portfolio)

Mode: Persuade. One page plus a page per blog post. Visitor: a hiring manager or recruiter from a job application, often on a phone, under a minute. Action: email Aidan or open the resume. Proof: create-db, the Prisma docs rebuild, docs for AI agents, the REST API docs, guides, OpenTape. Constraints in PRODUCT.md (copy bans, exact numbers, Prisma framing).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal site that reads like a short, well-set letter: name, three paragraphs, then tidy lists. It refuses the developer-portfolio template (hero headline, button row, card grid, stat tiles) and the concept-world costume.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white ground and near-black ink in light; inverted in dark, following the visitor. One italic serif voice (Spectral) for the name and section headings; the system UI sans for everything else. Links carry a soft marker highlight in a warm ochre, the one accent. No cards, boxes or icons; lists are title, one line, and a quiet right-aligned date or place, separated by hairline rules as on leerob.com (one of Aidan's two references). Code blocks in posts sit in a quiet tinted panel with a hairline edge, following the visitor's theme.

STORY: In one screen the visitor learns who Aidan is, what he built at Prisma and on his own, what roles he wants, and how to email him. Below: Work first (PRODUCT.md principle 1, best work within 30 seconds), then Experience, Writing, Guides (added at Aidan's request: every guide he wrote, as a compact link grid), and Open source. They email him or open the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On wide screens (Aidan asked to "make more use of the horiz space"), a sticky left column holds the intro while the lists scroll in a right column; on phones, one column. The italic serif name large at the top. Two to three bio paragraphs at reading size with highlighted links to Prisma, create-db, the docs rebuild and OpenTape. The last paragraph names the roles and carries the email link; a quiet line below holds Resume, GitHub, LinkedIn.

FORM: canon, the category standard played straight, at the craft level of evanyou.me and leerob.com (Aidan chose it after two hands). seed: seed:ff7561ee0c136e1b

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
